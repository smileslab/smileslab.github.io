Web filtering solutions, a vital component of cybersecurity, block access to
malicious websites, prevent malware from infecting machines, and protect
organizations' sensitive data. They offer a secure, efficient and controlled
online experience across sectors, addressing concerns related to security,
productivity and content appropriateness.

UM-Flint has partnered with leading Japanese URL filtering company Netstar Inc.
to develop a machine learning based solution. The team consists of PhD students
and postdoctoral researchers from the SMILES Lab together with Netstar
employees.

Data and knowledge sharing calls for dynamic classification of web content,
particularly at the edges of the Internet. The main purpose of URL
classification is to perform dynamic web filtering as the internet moves toward
end-to-end encryption. We employ multimodal and neuro-symbolic AI to classify
both encrypted and non-encrypted traffic, ensuring efficient categorization of
URLs.
