Disinformation has seen a distinctive rise in recent years, fueled by deepfaked
multimedia. From political leaders delivering fabricated speeches to manipulated
audio and video in scams, deepfakes pose a significant threat.

With research spanning over six years and nearly $1M in grants, we developed a
deepfake detector that identifies audio-visual forgeries across a range of
deepfake types. This NSF-PFI and MTRAC funded project seeks to further improve
the Deep Forgery Detector (DFD) technology.
