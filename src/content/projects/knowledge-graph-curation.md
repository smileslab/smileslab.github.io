Automated curation of a knowledge graph taken from voluminous neurological
unstructured data can extract valuable and actionable information. This
machine-readable information can support knowledge discovery from clinical data,
assist clinical decision making, and allow for the recommendation of
personalized treatment. It may also be used to understand unknown risk factors,
the natural history of complex diseases, and the effectiveness of different
treatments.

This project creates a novel framework to organize and integrate clinical notes
into conceptual knowledge graphs by employing semantic and statistical analysis
of clinical data.
