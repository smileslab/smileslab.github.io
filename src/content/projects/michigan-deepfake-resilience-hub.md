The Michigan Deepfake Resilience Hub (M-DRH) integrates advanced detection
with public literacy to safeguard democratic institutions. Dr. Khalid Malik is
Lead PI, with co-PIs Cliff Lampe (School of Information), Rada Mihalcea
(College of Engineering) and Hafiz Malik (UM-Dearborn). The project runs from
September 2026 to August 2029.

## Our approach

Detection tools are often too technical for everyday users. The hub combines
explainable visual and audio deepfake detection with interfaces that let
people examine the forensic evidence for themselves, alongside education and
person-first design.

## Goals

- A public verification portal for checking suspicious media.
- Training more than 1,000 "Digital Integrity Ambassadors".
- A response playbook and policy guidance for Michigan.
