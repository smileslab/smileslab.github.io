Dr. Khalid Mahmood Malik chaired a session at the IEEE International Joint
Conference on Biometrics (IJCB) 2026.

IJCB has been a regular venue for the lab's work on biometric security and
deepfake detection, including
[DFP-Net](https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcb57857.2023.10448842) (2023), and
[RuleBoost](https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcb62174.2024.10744498) and
[ConvNext-PNet](https://doi.org/10.1109/ijcb62174.2024.10744484) (2024).
