The [Michigan Deepfake Resilience Hub](https://looktomichigan.umich.edu/sif-projects/the-michigan-deepfake-resilience-hub/)
(M-DRH) is one of the University of Michigan's
[Look to Michigan](https://looktomichigan.umich.edu/) Strategic Initiative Fund
projects. Dr. Khalid Mahmood Malik of the UM-Flint College of Innovation and
Technology is the project's lead contact.

The hub brings together explainable AI, human-centered design and education to
help citizens, educators and public institutions spot AI-generated content and
protect themselves from AI-generated fraud. Many existing detection tools are
too technical for everyday users, and the hub is built to close that gap.

## Three pillars

- **Advanced technology:** detection that combines visual and audio analysis,
  with interactive interfaces that let people examine the forensic evidence for
  themselves.
- **Education:** curricula and linguistic training.
- **Person-first design:** tools shaped around the people who will use them.

## Goals

- A public verification portal for checking suspicious media.
- Training more than 1,000 "Digital Integrity Ambassadors".
- A response playbook and policy guidance for Michigan.

## The team

The hub spans three U-M campuses. Alongside the UM-Flint College of Innovation
and Technology, it includes Rada Mihalcea (College of Engineering), Hafiz Malik
(UM-Dearborn College of Engineering and Computer Science) and Cliff Lampe
(School of Information).
