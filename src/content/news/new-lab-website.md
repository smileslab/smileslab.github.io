Our website has been rebuilt from scratch. The previous site served us well, but
it had grown into a single page that search engines could not read: none of our
publications, projects or team members were indexable.

The new site fixes that, and adds a few things we have wanted for a while:

- **Every section is now its own page**, so you can link directly to a
  publication list, a project, or a person.
- **Publications are searchable and filterable** across all 55+ papers.
- **This news section**, where we will post awards, accepted papers, new
  collaborations and longer technical write-ups.
- **Much faster loading**, particularly on mobile. Images are now generated at
  the size they are actually displayed rather than shipped at full resolution.

If you spot something out of date or broken, please
[get in touch](/contact).
