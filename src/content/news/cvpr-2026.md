Members of the SMILES Lab attended the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision
and Pattern Recognition (CVPR) 2026. Two papers from the lab appeared in the
workshop programme.

## TRACE — training-free partial audio deepfake detection

**Awais Khan, Muhammad Umar Farooq, Kutub Uddin and Khalid Mahmood Malik**

TRACE detects partial audio deepfakes without any training, by analysing the
trajectory of embeddings through a frozen speech foundation model. The
intuition is that genuine speech produces smooth embedding trajectories, while
splice boundaries introduce abrupt disruptions.

[Read the paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/MMFM5/html/khan_TRACE_Training-Free_Partial_Audio_Deepfake_Detection_via_Embedding_Trajectory_Analysis_CVPRW_2026_paper.html)

## ARAN — artery-aware aneurysm detection

**Muzammal Shafique, Nasir Rahim, Muhammad Saad Saeed, Ghaus Malik and Khalid
Mahmood Malik**

ARAN leverages foundation models for vasculature-tree-informed, artery-aware
intracranial aneurysm detection across 3D CTA and ToF-MRA imaging.

[Read the paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/PHAROS-AIF-MIH/html/Shafique_ARAN_Leveraging_Foundation_Models_for_Vasculature-Tree-Informed_ARtery-Aware_Intracranial_ANeurysm_Detection_CVPRW_2026_paper.html)
