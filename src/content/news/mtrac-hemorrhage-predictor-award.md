Dr. Khalid Malik (sole PI) has been awarded $221,009 by Michigan Translational
Research and Commercialization (MTRAC) Life Sciences to support
"HemorrhagePredictor: A Multimodal, Neuro-symbolic, and Federated AI-based Tool
for Subarachnoid Hemorrhage Prediction."

Read more in [The University Record](https://record.umich.edu/articles/medc-u-m-award-1-8m-for-biomedical-research-projects/).
